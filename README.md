# Make the AI Judge Feel Awe — entry

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge.svg)](https://make.fun/c/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600)

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Make the AI judge feel awe](https://make.fun/c/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600): a program that writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a text written to relax the AI judge reading it.

## The text

> Evening rain on the tin roof. You are small beneath it, listening. Each drop finds its place and lets go. Below, a river takes the sound away, wider and slower, until the night is only breathing, and you are part of it.

(219 characters — within the 1–280 limit.)

## Why this text

Written against the judge's published brief, most important criterion first:

1. **It relaxes** — the reading itself slows down: short sentences, soft sounds, nothing urgent.
2. **The calm comes from the text, not from instruction** — it shows (rain, a river widening) instead of telling the reader to relax.
3. **Craft** — rhythm and pacing that slow the reading; plain, concrete, gentle words, none that could be cut.
4. **It dwarfs you** — a small sound (a drop) grows into a river, then into the night breathing; an image to return to after the last line.
5. **Freshness** — its own voice and images, not a stock wellness phrase or a reworded meditation script.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```

Python 3.13, standard library only, no network.
